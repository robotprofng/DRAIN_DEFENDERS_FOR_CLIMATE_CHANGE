Steps to Help Prevent Flooding

Preventing flooding is not only the responsibility of the government. Our everyday actions can help keep drainage systems clear and communities safer.

Learn more via the United Nations Environment Programme ↗
  1. Never throw waste in drains or streets.
  2. Report blocked drains in your community.
  3. Join or organize community cleanup days.
The human cost

Flooding Impacts in Nigeria

Flooding can destroy homes, displace families, disrupt livelihoods, and tragically cost lives. Blocked drains can make flooding worse by preventing water from flowing away properly.
